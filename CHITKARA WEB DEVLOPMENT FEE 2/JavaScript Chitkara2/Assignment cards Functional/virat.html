Virat Kohli

Early Life and Background:
Virat Kohli was born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, India. He developed an early interest in cricket, and his talent was recognized at a young age. He trained at the West Delhi Cricket Academy and was soon making waves in junior cricket circuits.

Domestic Career:
Kohli's domestic career began with his debut for Delhi in 2006. He quickly rose through the ranks, making significant contributions in the domestic circuit. His standout performance came when he captained the Indian U-19 team to victory in the 2008 ICC U-19 Cricket World Cup.

International Career:
Debut and Early Years:
Kohli made his international debut for India in an ODI against Sri Lanka on August 18, 2008. His performances in the early years were solid, but he truly came into his own during the 2011 ICC Cricket World Cup, where he was one of India's top performers.

Rise to Stardom:
Over the years, Kohli established himself as one of the best batsmen in the world across all formats. He has broken numerous records, including being the fastest to 8,000, 9,000, 10,000, 11,000, and 12,000 runs in ODIs. Kohli's aggressive style and consistent performances have earned him the nickname "King Kohli."

Captaincy:
Kohli took over as captain of the Indian cricket team in all formats in 2017, following the retirement of MS Dhoni. Under his leadership, India achieved significant milestones, including historic Test series wins in Australia and dominance at home.

Personal Life:
Virat Kohli married Bollywood actress Anushka Sharma in December 2017. The couple is known for their philanthropic efforts and have a daughter named Vamika, born in January 2021.

Achievements and Awards:

  • ICC Cricketer of the Year: 2017, 2018
  • Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna: 2018
  • Padma Shri: 2017
  • Wisden Leading Cricketer in the World: 2016, 2017, 2018

Legacy:
Kohli is widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen of his generation. His impact on the game, both as a player and a leader, is profound, and he continues to be a key figure in Indian cricket.
